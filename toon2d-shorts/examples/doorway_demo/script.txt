Mr. Black stands up from his desk, walks to the kitchen, and freezes.
He has no idea why he came.
He checks his pockets. He checks the sky.
He walks back, sits down, and instantly remembers: the key.
You see, your brain files memories by room, and stepping through a doorway closes the file.
Ever walk into the kitchen and forget why?
Researchers at Notre Dame found people forgot more after passing through a doorway than after walking the same distance inside one room.
It's called the doorway effect.
Your brain thinks a new scene means a new chapter.
So next time, don't stand there.
Walk back to where the thought began.
